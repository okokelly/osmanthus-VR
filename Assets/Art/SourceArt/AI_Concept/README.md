# Osmanthus VR - AI Concept Art

This folder contains the first visual-development pass generated with the built-in GPT Image workflow.

The exact generation and refinement prompts are recorded in `PROMPTS.md`.

## Color script

### Scene 1 - Entering Memory

- First-person, eye-level VR viewpoint.
- Fragmented Summer Palace Long Corridor with missing roof, beams, and columns.
- Cool blue-gray fog and desaturated architecture dominate the frame.
- A sparse osmanthus-gold trail is the only strong warm cue.
- Avoid an intact palace, an amber wash, and dense particles.

### Scene 2 - Pillar Awakening

- Continue the same corridor, camera, scale, and cool atmosphere.
- Localize the warm response to the touched pillar and one continuous cinematic memory canvas.
- Darken the surrounding corridor so the memory takes over the user's attention.
- Unfold one large thin video surface from the contact point instead of spawning multiple floating frames.
- Use a shaped single-sided mesh or plane with a video material; the video content does not need to become a 3D scene.
- Keep the screen borderless, gently curved, and visually connected to the pillar by a restrained particle stream.

### Scene 3 - Reconstruction

- Reveal the lake from the broken end of the corridor.
- Interlock horizontal traditional timber/eave fragments with vertical cold-white contemporary structures.
- Let the two systems interrupt and reshape each other instead of placing a skyline behind a garden.
- Retain unjoined edges, cool fog, and cold city light within the warmer final state.
- Use osmanthus as connective tissue between structural gaps.
- Use recognizable city-memory anchors rather than anonymous towers: Hong Kong diagonal high-rise geometry, the Eiffel Tower, Elizabeth Tower, and the Empire State Building.
- Keep each landmark partial and integrated into the reconstructed garden so the result reads as identity, not travel advertising.
- Anchor the old-world architecture in the real Summer Palace Long Corridor: long low open-sided bays, slender green lacquered posts, red geometric railings, gray tiled roof, and repeated Su-style painted beam panels.
- Treat a pavilion as a secondary node within the promenade, never as the dominant old-world building.
- Restore fragmentation by removing selected bays and roof sections after the Long Corridor identity is legible.

## Shared visual constraints

- Standalone Meta Quest target; use large readable forms and restrained transparency.
- Unity URP-friendly look based on opaque materials, controlled emission, fog cards, and limited memory planes.
- No perfume bottle, advertising language, logo, text, cyberpunk neon, portal effects, or full palace restoration.
- Treat these images as art-direction targets, not literal asset sheets or historically authoritative references.
